Android Instructions

Due to Android 11+ security on Android/data, use manual copy or Share to load scores:

  1. Method 1 (Copy to Download): In your file explorer, navigate to Android/data/com.Reflektone.AstroDX/files/cache, tap Copy, and paste it into your Download folder. Then tap "Select Cache File" to load!
  2. Method 2 (Instant Share): In your file explorer, select the cache file, tap Share, and choose AstroDX Score Exporter.
  3. Why? Modern Android (11+) isolates Android/data for security, so direct background reading is restricted by the OS unless copied or shared.

iOS 1-Tap Auto-Sync Setup

Extract your AstroDX scores directly with 1 tap from your Home Screen

On iPad or iPhone, Apple's built-in Shortcuts (快捷指令) app has permission to read the AstroDX folder. By creating a 4-step shortcut on your Home Screen, you never have to manually browse the Files app again!

1

Open the "Shortcuts" (快捷指令) App

Tap + to create a new shortcut, and name it "Sync AstroDX".

2

Add 4 Simple Actions in Order

Action 1
File (文件) → Select On My iPad/iPhone → AstroDX → cache
(Toggle off "Show Document Picker")
Action 2
Base64 Encode (Base64 编码) → Input: File, Line Breaks: None
Action 3
URL (网址) → Set to:
https://...#b64=
In the URL box, tap right after #b64= and insert the [Base64 Encoded Text] variable from Action 2.
Action 4
Open URLs (打开 URL) → Input: URL from Action 3
3

Add to Home Screen & Tap to Run!

Tap the share icon in Shortcuts → "Add to Home Screen". Whenever you want to export your scores, just tap the icon!

💡
Clipboard Backup (For Very Large Databases):

You can also add a "Copy to Clipboard" action in your shortcut, and click "Paste from Clipboard" here to load instantly without URL length limitations.
